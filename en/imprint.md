### Weather data

The weather values POTD writes into a photo - and which appear in the screenshots on this website -
come from [Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com) and are licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Sun position, golden and blue hour and shadow length, by contrast, are computed by the app itself
