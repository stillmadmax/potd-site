# Support for POTD (Pic of the Day)

POTD captions your photos with capture data such as place, date and weather - right on the iPhone,
without your images ever leaving the device.

## Contact

Questions, problems or feedback? Write to
[potd.picoftheday@gmail.com](mailto:potd.picoftheday@gmail.com).

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is a text field empty?

POTD only shows data that is actually there - it never guesses. An empty field means the photo does
not contain the necessary information. The most common case: the image has no GPS coordinates (for
example screenshots, images saved from messengers, or shots taken with location access switched
off). Without coordinates, place, weather, peak and the sun fields cannot be determined.

### Where does the weather for older photos come from?

For photos from your library, POTD requests the historical weather for the time of capture - this
requires both a capture date and coordinates in the photo. Only for pictures taken directly with the
camera inside the app is the current weather used.

### What exactly does the peak field mean?

It shows the **nearest named peak** to the place of capture - which is not necessarily the mountain
visible in the photo.

### Is my original photo modified?

No. The captioned image is always saved as a new photo in your library; the original stays
unchanged.

### Can I share an image without the location?

Yes. If the image carries location data, POTD asks when sharing whether to include it or not.

### How do I reset the app?

In the app settings under "Reset app to default settings". This removes all templates, styles and
settings and restores the state the app shipped with - your photos in the library are left
untouched.

### Why does the app ask for permissions?

- **Photos**: to select a photo and save the result
- **Camera**: only if you take pictures inside the app
- **Location**: only for camera shots, to determine place and weather for "here and now"

Details are in the [privacy policy](privacy).
